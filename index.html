About Padma Refuge

A foundation for the Dhamma, held by many hands.

Padma Refuge Foundation is a Canadian registered charitable organization dedicated to supporting Buddhist teachings, monastic training, meditation and spiritual development.

Rooted in the Theravāda tradition and the teachings of Gautama Buddha, Padma Refuge provides an organizational foundation through which monastics, practitioners, volunteers and community members can come together to preserve and share the Dhamma.

Through spiritual education, meditation, retreats, devotional practice and community initiatives, we seek to make the teachings of the Buddha accessible to people from all walks of life.

Our work is grounded in the understanding that the preservation of the Dhamma depends upon the relationship between the Buddha, the teachings, the Sangha and the generosity of the lay community.

The First Jewel

Buddha

The Awakened One, whose example shows that freedom from suffering is possible.

The Second Jewel

Dhamma

The teachings, offered freely and preserved through study, practice and transmission.

The Third Jewel

Sangha

The monastic community who live, embody and carry the teachings forward.

What sustains them

Dāna

The generosity of the lay community, which makes the life of the Sangha and the sharing of the Dhamma possible.

Our Charitable Purpose

Padma Refuge is registered to carry out three charitable purposes. We fulfil them through monastic support, meditation, Dhamma education, retreats and community engagement.

  1. 01Operating a Buddhist house of worship.
  2. 02Teaching Buddhist tenets and observances.
  3. 03Undertaking ancillary charitable activities.
