Generosity is the first practice.

The Sangha has always been sustained by the generosity of the lay community. Whether you offer a candle, a monthly gift or an afternoon in the garden, you take part in preserving the Dhamma.

Make an Offering

A traditional way to express gratitude and devotion. Offerings may be made in honour or memory of a loved one.

Buddha OfferingsFood, water and gifts offered at the shrine. Candles & LightsLight dispels darkness, as wisdom dispels ignorance. Incense & FlowersFragrance and impermanent beauty, offered with care.
